## The meta-thesis (your sweet spot)

Your rarest position is the **intersection of deep physical-world domain expertise and AI-native building ability.** AI-native founders lack your domain depth; domain experts can't ship AI. You're one of the few who can stand in both. **So bias every weekly hunt toward the AI-×-domain wedge** — vertical AI for supply chain / cold chain / CPG / industrial ops — rather than horizontal AI infra (where your edge is thin) or pure physical-ops plays (where AI gives you no advantage). That intersection is where your moat and your unfair right to win overlap.
