# P1 — US food/CPG contract manufacturing: market structure & buyer economics

**Date:** 2026-07-26 · **Sources:** web sweep this date; all claims carry inline URLs or `[UNVERIFIED]`.

## Market size & growth

- US food contract manufacturing & services market: **$138B (2024) → projected $303B by 2032–33, ~10.3% CAGR** (https://www.openpr.com/news/4468870/food-contract-manufacturing-services-market-to-reach-us). *Caveat: report-mill grade; treat as order-of-magnitude only.*
- Global food contract manufacturing: **$404B (2025) → $692B by 2034, 6.2% CAGR** (https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/food-contract-manufacturing-market-115888). Conflicts with the above on level and growth — recorded as a range per repo rules: **US market plausibly $100–150B, growth 6–12%/yr.**
- Contract Packaging Association 2025 State of the Industry (9th ed., 200+ executive interviews — the credible primary source; worth buying): sector growing at **11.9% CAGR over the past five years, 2–4x faster than the industries it serves**, alongside heavy consolidation pressure (https://www.contractpackaging.org/state-of-the-industry-report, https://www.packworld.com/trends/contract-packaging/article/13375428/copackcoman-industry-is-vibrant-strong-and-growing).

**Read:** the market is large and growing faster than the brands it serves — outsourced production is taking share of total production. That is the demand-side why-now.

## How many co-mans (H1 — India↔US structural difference)

- PartnerSlate marketplace database: **~6,000 US/NA food & CPG co-manufacturers** (https://www.foodnavigator-usa.com/Article/2023/06/12/PartnerSlate-connects-food-brands-with-co-manufacturers-raises-4-million-in-funding/).

## Outsourcing share (demand side)

- **63% of CPG brand owners outsource 10–49% of production; 67% plan to maintain or increase** contract-services use; only 19% plan to reduce (CPA survey via https://www.packworld.com/trends/contract-packaging/article/22938542/sunny-outlook-for-outsourcing-external-manufacturing).
- Private-label boom is pulling co-man volume (retailer brands are made almost entirely by co-mans) (https://tracegains.com/blog/co-manufacturers-ride-private-label-sales-wave/).

## Margins & paying capacity (H2)

- Food-manufacturing businesses trade at **4–8x EBITDA**; co-packers running PO-by-PO with no term agreements take the deepest valuation discounts; customer concentration and aging lines pull multiples down (https://ctacquisitions.com/guides/food-manufacturing-business-valuation/, https://dealstream.com/industry-guides/food-manufacturing-businesses/rules-of-thumb).
- **No direct sourced EBITDA-margin benchmark for food co-mans found this sweep** — general manufacturing medians (~17–18% EBITDA/sales, https://manufacturingleadgeneration.com/manufacturing-financial-benchmarks/) are not co-man-specific. `[UNVERIFIED — co-man margin range is a P5 interview target: ask owners directly]`
- Software spend evidence (they do pay): Nulogy contracts run **~$80k–250k/yr** for brand-owner deployments (https://logicatalog.com/vendors/nulogy/ — medium credibility); co-man site licenses plausibly lower `[UNVERIFIED]`. Food-specific ERPs (Deacom/ECI, Aptean, Wherefour) are an established purchase for mid-size co-mans (https://wherefour.com/best-food-beverage-manufacturing-erp-software/, https://www.ecisolutions.com/industries/manufacturing/erp-software-for-contract-manufacturing/). MaintainX serves 11k+ companies including F&B plants (https://www.getmaintainx.com/newsroom/maintainx-raises-150m).

**H2 read:** *directionally supported* — a real vendor ecosystem (Nulogy 20+ yrs, food ERPs, CMMS) exists and gets paid five-to-six figures annually per account. Magnitude per plant and budget owner: interview question, not desk-solvable.

## Why-now vectors (candidate — each needs kill-testing in P3)

1. **Outsourcing share rising + private label wave** (sources above).
2. **FSMA 204 traceability rule — compliance deadline July 20, 2028** hits food manufacturers incl. co-mans (https://www.alleratech.com/blog/food-safety-software-guide). `[verify current deadline status directly at FDA at P3]`
3. **Consolidation/PE roll-ups** in co-pack/co-man (CPA SOIR theme) → professionalizing buyers with budgets.
4. **AI cost curve** — document-heavy, tribal-knowledge-heavy workflows (quoting, spec intake, FSQA docs, audit prep) newly automatable; proven in adjacent segments (Paperless Parts in job shops, Glimpse in CPG deductions).
5. **Labor scarcity** on lines and in QA/maintenance roles (carried from F&B run: 622k unfilled US mfg jobs `[re-verify]`).
