## Structural findings (recovered)
- **US pattern:** every legible US software cluster was occupied by funded 2023–25 entrants — Confido, TrewUp, Specright, TraceGains, ReposiTrak, Afresh, Formic, Augury, Trax/FORM. Only one US problem cleared ADVANCE cleanly.
- **India survivor pattern:** regulatory forcing function + fragmented incumbents + founder operating scar tissue.
- **Why-now vectors that recurred:** DMS/e-invoicing mandates digitizing channel data; GST 2.0 volume effects; MOP channel war (GT vs q-commerce) at peak; q-commerce SKU proliferation shortening runs; FSSAI enforcement surge; PLI-driven processing-capacity build; China-plus-one volumes to Indian CMs. `[from record — verify current status before reuse]`
- **Recovered deep-dive detail (S5-IN-01):** brand-side DMS incumbents (FieldAssist, Bizom, Botree) serve the *brand's* claims workflow; no distributor-side cross-brand claims platform found — a distributor juggling 5–15 brands' schemes has no tool. Wedge: distributor-side claims cockpit (parse scheme circulars, auto-file, track ageing) via distributor associations; genuine counter-positioning (DMS vendors can't take the distributor's side without channel conflict). Riskiest assumption: distributors' WTP vs expecting brands to fund it.
- **Recovered deep-dive detail (S5-IN-03):** e-com price monitoring exists (MetricsCart, Paxcom); nobody does cross-channel price truth incl. GT capture; GT price data network = the differentiator and the riskiest assumption.
